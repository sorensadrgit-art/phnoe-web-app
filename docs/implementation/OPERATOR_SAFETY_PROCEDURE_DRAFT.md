## 2. Integrated Stop activity: source behavior and verification limits

The current candidate provides a text-labelled `Stop activity` action in the live view and inside the summary dialog. Its handlers do not depend on recording-busy or save completion. Source sets a synchronous stop latch before asynchronous storage work, clears the current observation and disables visual tracking/coaching. It checks the latch before late camera acquisition or inference results can promote activity, and before a delayed model hash/create or save retry can resume synthetic sampling. The latch lasts for the current mount: `Resume tracking`, new recording, camera start and `Start New Set` remain disabled. Do not reload or navigate to bypass it. Any separate activity requires its own reviewed case after the previous record state is resolved or retained.

Use `Stop activity` to end the affected generated activity. Read its status: `Activity stopped. Follow the owner-approved protocol; clinical next-step approval is PENDING. Recording saved/pending status is shown separately.` This neutral message does not supply a clinical next-step protocol. The action attempts cleanup of owned camera tracks, inference work, animation and sound resources. This procedure authorizes no camera or physical inference exercise. Physical camera/audio/inference shutdown and numeric stop latency remain NOT_RUN. This bounded result does not establish the broader keyboard-only workflow, native zoom, representative-operator or physical-device acceptance.

Saving proceeds separately. Stop prevents new recording samples immediately in source, while an existing create, chunk or finalization operation may still settle. Produced frames are flushed/finalized through the existing recording contract when possible. An empty created draft is not finalized. A failed or uncertain operation retains the draft and pending bytes; an acknowledgment is required before claiming a saved prefix.

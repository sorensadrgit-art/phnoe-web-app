# SOREN section studies

## Style Prompt
Six precise moving objects inside the existing ivory editorial résumé cards. Dark ink, warm paper, surgical teal and restrained copper connect movement science and creative engineering. Each object expresses its section without implying measured clinical results or invented experience.

## Colors
- Ink #10282b: background
- Paper #f1eee8: primary type and lines
- Teal #88c9bd: motion paths
- Copper #d5aa7d: active points
- Muted #acc2bc: secondary labels

## Typography
Georgia for editorial headings; Arial for labels; Consolas for technical notation. Preserve the résumé's existing fonts.

## Motion
Six-second seamless silent studies. Continuous mechanical motion with a calm visual center. No flashing, invented metrics, simulated medical measurements, or stock photography. Static poster when reduced motion is requested. Existing ribbon physics remain unchanged.

## What NOT to Do
- Invent credentials, dates, measured outcomes or contact details.
- Add dashboard clutter or text too small to read.
- Replace the ivory card system or exact ribbon motion.
- Use generic image zooms as the section animation.
